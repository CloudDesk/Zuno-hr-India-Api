# ⏸️ Final Settlement (FNF) - Hold Payroll Identification Guide

This guide explains how the system identifies and processes **Hold Payrolls** during the Final Settlement (FNF) workflow. This information is critical for frontend confirmation and audit.

---

## 🔍 1. How the Backend Finds Hold Payrolls

The backend searches the `Payroll` collection for specific records that meet the following criteria:

- **Employee Match**: The `employeeId` matches the person undergoing final settlement.
- **Status Filter**: The `status` of the payroll record is exactly **`Hold`**.
- **Sorting**: Records are sorted chronologically by `year` and `month` to ensure the sequence of payment is clear.

### The Database Query (Technical)
```typescript
const holdPayrolls = await Payroll.find({
    employeeId: new Types.ObjectId(employeeId),
    status: 'Hold'
}).sort({ year: 1, month: 1 });
```

## 📊 2. Frontend Mapping (API Response)

When the frontend calls the **Initialize API** (`GET /final-settlement/initialize/:id`), the backend returns an array called `holdPayrolls`.

## ⚖️ 3. Hold Payroll vs. Unpaid Month

It is important to distinguish these two items in the UI:

1.  **Hold Payroll**:
    - **Status**: Already calculated.
    - **Source**: Exists in the `Payroll` collection.
    - **Action**: FNF simply "releases" this pre-calculated amount.
2.  **Unpaid Month (Gap)**:
    - **Status**: Not yet calculated.
    - **Source**: Does **not** exist in the `Payroll` collection.
    - **Action**: FNF performs a fresh **pro-rata calculation** based on actual attendance/LWD.

---

## 🚀 4. Final Processing Logic

When the settlement is **Confirmed** (`POST /final-settlement/confirm/:id`):

- The backend iterates through all linked Hold Payrolls.
- It updates their status from **`Hold`** to **`Completed`**.
- It stamps the `paymentConfirmedAt` date as the date of settlement confirmation.
- These records are then excluded from future "Hold" reports as they are now officially paid through the FNF.
